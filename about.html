About 'Anah'.

Ever tackled a mountain slope at sunrise, feeling the thrill of the unknown, yet completely confident in your skills? That's how I approach my life as well as design challenges—excited, prepared, and ready to learn and carve out innovative paths.

My journey began in Kharagpur, India, where a passion for design clashed with the view of art as a hobby. I studied civil engineering to build an analytical foundation, but my drive for creative problem-solving led me to product design.

My design thinking has evolved through hands-on experience across diverse domains—streamlining food pantry operations for NGOs, conceptualizing AI-driven tools for first responders, and building 0-to-1 products that balance user needs with technical feasibility.

The other half.

When I'm not scaling design challenges, you can find me cheering on Real Madrid (don't worry, work comes first ! ). I'm also an avid cook 🍽️; a digital artist 🎨; a coffee person ☕; a badminton 🏸and football ⚽ enthusiast (yes, both versions! ); and a passionate explorer of new cities. 🌇

Photography helps me unveil hidden stories in everyday scenes, often overlooked 👁️ and ordinary, while video games keep my competitive spirit⚡sharp. And last but not least, reading articles keeps me updated.

These hobbies fuel my creativity and offer fresh perspectives on design. 🪄
